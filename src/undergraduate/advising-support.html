Advising matters for undergraduate Psychology students are split between the Psychology Undergraduate Advising Office and the ACCESS Advising Office. The duties of each office are as follows:

PSY Undergraduate Advising Office

  • Advising for all Psychology majors.
  • Psychology major course requirements advising.
  • Multiple Major Applications.
    • *This form does not come to ACCESS if the second major is outside of CSS*
  • Review of Psychology major progress prior to filing for graduation with ACCESS.
  • Career and graduate school advice. The Undergraduate Chair also provides seminars and meets with students about this topic.
  • Psychology-specific internship and research assistantship guidance.

ACCESS Advising Office

  • Degree advising (including general education) for Pre-PSY and PSY majors.
  • Prospective student inquiries.
  • Discussing all administrative forms (e.g., VA, ROTC, SAP, etc.).
  • Discussing requests for Late Adds, Withdrawals, and Leave of Absence.
  • Resolving Holds due to Mandatory Advising.
  • Discussing Petitions.
  • Discussing and Referrals for information about scholarships, internships, and special educational programs.

Students who intend to enter either the BA or BS degree program must first declare Psychology as their major. In order to declare, the following pre-psychology requirements must be met:

Required Pre-Psychology Courses

  1. PSY 100: Survey of Psychology
  2. PSY 212: Survey of Research Methods
  3. PSY 225: Statistical Techniques or SOCS 225: Statistical Analysis for Social Sciences

Other statistics courses may be used to meet the PSY 225 or SOCS 225 requirement.Please check with the Psychology Undergraduate Advising Office.

Psychology Grade Point Average (GPA)

To declare Psychology as a major, students must have a minimum Psychology GPA of 2.50, which includes Psychology transfer courses.

Attendance of a Psychology Major Meeting

You must attend one Psychology Major Meeting. These are large orientation meetings held over Zoom in the Spring and Fall semesters, and provide important information about your degree, academic opportunities, and department resources. You can find the schedule for this semester's meetings on our website.

Submission of Declaration of Major Form

Once you've completed all the requirements, you can declare your major through this online Kuali form. Major declarations are processed in 2-3 weeks. Please note: Major declarations aren't processed over the summer unless there are special circumstances (e.g., VA or athletics).

Please use our secure Kuali Document Submission Form (UH Login required) to submit documents to our office. For your protection and to ensure your security, we no longer accept email attachments.

Simply select which type of document(s) you are submitting, attach your document(s), click "Submit," and we will review your submission as soon as possible.

All graduation forms and procedures described below must be completed during the semester that the student plans to graduate.

  1. Students meet with an advisor in the Psychology Undergraduate Advising Office to review the Psychology Major course requirements and determine if they have met the Psychology Major requirements.
  2. Students complete an Exit Survey for the Department of Psychology.
  3. Students then schedule an appointment with an ACCESS Advisor to file for graduation and address questions related to graduation.
