Psi Sigma: The Psychology Connection

Psi Sigma is a social club for psychology majors at UH Mānoa which:

  • Encourages the pursuit of psychological science.
  • Fosters enduring connections among peers in psychology.
  • Advances the academic and professional development of our members.
  • For full club details, admissions, and contact details visit the Psi Sigma website.
