Research Project Summary for Psychology BS Degree

At least one of the PSY 499: Directed Research courses used to fulfill the BS requirements must involve direct research experience in a professor’s lab. Ideally, students should complete two semesters in the same lab, but this is not required. Students must submit a summary of their research involvement. The summary needs to be no more than two pages, APA style not required.

  • Describe your role in the research project.
  • Describe the research hypothesis.
  • What results did you find?
  • Describe the significance and implications of the results.

Additional notes:

  • No grade is given and neither yours nor your supervisor’s signature is required.
  • Due a month prior to the end of your semester of graduation.

Submit via email to Dr. Sinnett (ssinnett@hawaii.edu)
