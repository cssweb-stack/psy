Applying for Graduate Study

The admissions process is highly competitive. Prospective students are strongly encouraged to review the prospective student information webpage and to familiarize themselves with our faculty. We welcome applications from individuals whose academic interests match our expertise.

  • Applications for Fall 2027 admissions are OPEN.
  • The application deadline is Tuesday, December 1, 2026.
  • We strongly encourage submitting all application materials by Friday, November 20, 2026.
  • Early submissions allow sufficient time to address application issues before the deadline.
  • Incomplete applications will not be considered.

Special Notes

  • Please refer to the individual Graduate Program Areas for a list of faculty members accepting new students.
  • We no longer require GRE General test scores. In addition, the Community, Cultural, and Developmental program and Cognition, Neuroscience, and Social program do not require GRE Subject test scores. The GRE is optional for the Psychological Clinical Science program. If submitted, it will be considered holistically in conjunction with other materials, with no strict cutoff scores applied.

Application Process

The application process is divided into three stages. The stages can be completed concurrently.

For admission consideration, all three stages must be completed by the application deadline.

Applicants must complete the online application as provided by Graduate Division (see Graduate Admissions). We strongly encourage careful review of the application requirements. The following items must be submitted as part of the application:

  1. Transcripts (unofficial accepted)
  2. GRE Test (optional for the Clinical Science Program only)
  3. OfficialTOEFL/IELTS scores (English language proficiency, international students only)
  4. Application fee
  5. Three Recommendation Contacts (names and emails)

The Psychology Department only offers a doctoral program (with no terminal MA degree); however, applicants must select their program application level on the Graduate Division Application based on prior academic degrees.

Please review the criteria and flowchart below to ensure you select the correct program level on your application.

  1. You should apply to the Psychology-MA level if:
    • You do not currently hold an MA/MS in Psychology*
    • NOTE: This is to register you to complete a master's "en passant" on the way to the Ph.D.
  2. You should apply to the Psychology-PhD level if:
    • You have already completed an MA/MS degree in Psychology
    • NOTE: If you did not complete an empirical master's project in your MA/MS program, you should still apply to the Psychology-PhD level, but you will be asked to complete and defend an empirical project milestone before proceeding in the program.

*Those applicants with a master's degree (MA/MS) in a field closely related to psychology (e.g., neuroscience, sociology, cognitive science, counseling, social work) must contact our graduate chair, Dr. Kristin Pauker, at kpauker@hawaii.edu before submitting an application.

  • View program selection chart.

Please contact gradpsy@hawaii.edu if you have additional questions regarding program tracks, or the graduate chair, Dr. Kristin Pauker, if you need assistance determining your program application level.

Applicants must complete the Department of Psychology - Graduate Program Application specific to their intended program area. Please complete an application for either:

  • Cognition, Neuroscience, & Social (CNS) / Community, Cultural, and Developmental (CCD), or
  • Psychological Clinical Science Program (PCS)

The following items must be submitted as part of the application:

  1. Statement of Objectives (not required for the Psychological Clinical Science Program (PCS))
  2. Curriculum vitae (CV)
  3. Transcripts (unofficial accepted)
  4. Self-Reported Standardized Test Scores - TOEFL (if applicable), GRE Test is optional for the Psychological Clinical Science Program only.

Applicants should gather all the required materials before starting the application.

To be considered complete, each application must include three letters of recommendation. Academic references are preferred.

Applicants must direct each reference to complete the recommendation form for their intended program area. References complete a form for either:

  • Cognition, Neuroscience, & Social (CNS) / Community, Cultural, and Developmental (CCD), or
  • Psychological Clinical Science Program (PCS).

Applicants should share these links with their recommenders when they input their names/emails into the graduate division application via the directions supplied in the application.

We include the links here, in case applicants need to resend the link to their recommender. The applicant and their recommender will receive a confirmation email once the recommender has submitted the form.

Applicants are strongly encouraged to notify their references via the graduate division application by November 15.

Important:

  • Letters should not be mailed or emailed to Graduate Division or to the Department of Psychology.

Application Questions

  • Questions on STAGE 1 regarding program application level (Psychology-MA or Psychology-PhD) should be directed to gradpsy@hawaii.edu; all other questions on STAGE 1 should be directed to Graduate Division.(opens in new tab)
  • Questions on STAGE 2 and 3 should be directed to the Department of Psychology at gradpsy@hawaii.edu.

Graduate Program Admissions Timeline

  • December 1 - Deadline for all application materials.
  • Mid-February to early March - Applicants notified of an admission decision. In some cases, an applicant may be wait-listed, in which case final notification of acceptance or denial may not occur until early April.
  • April 15 - Deadline for applicants to accept or reject the admission.

All inquiries regarding graduate study should be directed to gradpsy@hawaii.edu.
