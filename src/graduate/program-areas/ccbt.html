Center for Cognitive Behavior Therapy

The Center for Cognitive Behavior Therapy (CCBT) is a training, service and research university-based clinic housed in the Department of Psychology at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa. The CCBT provides practicum training to UH Psychology Clinical Science Program graduate students and post-doctoral residents, under the supervision of Department faculty members who are also licensed clinical psychologists in Hawai'i. Consulting and clinical assessment and intervention services are provided to clients, their families and community organizations. CCBT research has contributed to the advancement of knowledge at the local, national and international level.

Child Section

The Child Section of the CCBT (C-CCBT) currently offers psychological assessment and treatment services for youth and their families. The C-CCBT has special expertise in the psychosocial treatment of childhood mental health difficulties, including anxiety, depression, trauma, inattention/hyperactivity, and oppositional behaviors. Under the supervision of directors and licensed psychologists Brad Nakamura and Daniel Wilkie, C-CCBT graduate students, pre-licensed therapists, and post-doctoral fellows provide all intervention and consultation services, including training presentations, assessment services, child therapy, and parent education and training in both individual and group formats. These evidence-based services can be delivered in the clinic and/or at the youth's home, school, or community (see the State of Hawaiʻi's Help Your Keiki website for more information on youth problems and supported treatment approaches). Assessments and treatment arranged by the State of Hawaiʻi Departments of Education or Health are free for clients. Self-referred clients are offered a sliding fee scale on a limited basis as available. No third party insurance is accepted.

Developmental Disabilities Assessment

The Developmental Disabilities Assessment (DD-CCBT) clinic is under the supervision of Dr. Masuda. Students who participate receive training to conduct psychological assessments that support the State of Hawaii Department of Health Developmental Disabilities Division (DDD) eligibility determinations for individuals applying for DDD services. Within this practicum, evaluate applicants' diagnoses and level of severity, typically including behavior observation, clinical interviews, cognitive assessment, and evaluation of the level of adaptive functioning.

The Developmental Disabilities Assessment (DD-CCBT) clinic does not take direct referrals. Instead, please contact the State of Hawaii Department of Health, Developmental Disabilities Division.

Eating Disorders

The Eating Disorders Clinic (ED-CCBT) is a specialty clinic focused on disordered eating, obesity, and body image dissatisfaction. The ED-CCBT is staffed by individuals with MA or PhD degrees in clinical psychology and supervised by UH faculty, providing specialized training in the eating disorder field for advanced graduate students.

The Eating Disorders clinic is not accepting outside referrals at this time.
